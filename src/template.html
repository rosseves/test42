Write ingredients the way you'd say them: 150g chicken breast, 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 eggs, 200ml semi skimmed milk. Use Alt+Enter for a new line inside a cell. If you fill in the macro columns we'll compare them to the ingredients and flag any disagreement.

  1. Start from the template. It has the right columns and three worked examples to type over.
  2. One recipe per row. Ingredients go one per line inside the single Ingredients cell — press Alt + Enter for a new line (Ctrl + Option + Enter on a Mac).
  3. Servings is what the amounts make, not what one person eats. Twelve energy balls means 12.
  4. Leave the macro columns blank. They are worked out from the ingredients. Fill them in only if you want them checked against.
  5. Bring it in — choose the file below, or just copy the rows in Excel and paste them in. Pasting works, and saves converting to CSV.
  6. Check before importing. Anything unrecognised is flagged and can be fixed here. Nothing is saved until you press Import.
